# Chapter 0 — The Last Signal

Playable prologue script and implementation notes.

## Story premise

Technology has become inseparable from war; humanity can manufacture robots across many worlds. The system once entrusted these machines to humans. It asks the pilot to retrieve them across the multiverse. Confirmed retrievals provide resources and robot-control authorization. Valhalla receives temporary emergency authorization in the field, then permanent access after returning to the Ark. Mender is retrieved as a conscious service robot who becomes the Ark’s first resident.

## opening

**COMMAND:** All civilian transports: break formation. We cannot hold this sector.

**PILOT:** Valhalla, keep us between them and the evacuation ship.

**VALHALLA:** Escape probability is declining.

**PILOT:** Then stop calculating mine. Find theirs.

## escape

**TRANSPORT:** Jump drive online. We are clear! You did it!

**PILOT:** Good. Now find us a way out.

**VALHALLA:** Incoming mass-driver signature. Impact in—

**PILOT:** Valhalla?

**VALHALLA:** Pilot—stay with—

## awakening

**PILOT:** ...Valhalla?

**PILOT:** No wreckage. No stars. Where is this?

**PILOT:** Valhalla, respond.

**SYSTEM:** No linked machine detected.

**PILOT:** Who said that?

## beacon

**SYSTEM:** You are inside an interstitial sanctuary.

**PILOT:** Am I dead?

**SYSTEM:** Your previous location is inaccessible.

**PILOT:** That is not an answer.

**SYSTEM:** Approach the central core. I can explain the signal.

## core

**SYSTEM:** Humanity made technology inseparable from war. Factories on countless worlds now create robots without limit.

**PILOT:** And this place?

**SYSTEM:** I gave humanity the machines that made that expansion possible. I ask one thing in return: retrieve the robots I entrusted to them.

**PILOT:** Every world?

**SYSTEM:** Across the multiverse. Each confirmed retrieval earns resources and authorization to control that robot. This Ark is their return point.

**PILOT:** Find someone else.

**SYSTEM:** There is no other operator.

**SIGNAL:** ...GX-01... link failure... pilot...

**PILOT:** Play that again.

**SYSTEM:** The signature matches Valhalla. Identity cannot be confirmed. It may be another version, or corrupted data.

**PILOT:** Open the gate.

## gate

**SYSTEM:** World 01: a silent orbital maintenance facility. The signal originates inside.

**PILOT:** If there is any chance that is Valhalla, I am going.

**SYSTEM:** Anchor established. Return while the connection remains stable.

## corridor

**PILOT:** Someone left in a hurry. The emergency lights are still on.

**SYSTEM:** A terminal ahead retains a partial evacuation record.

## record

**RECORD:** Evacuation order: reactor instability. Suspend all nonessential maintenance operations.

**RECORD:** Unaccounted units: GX-01 combat frame. Service unit M-03.

**PILOT:** They left two machines behind.

**SYSTEM:** The Valhalla signature is beyond the next bulkhead.

## maintenance

**PILOT:** There you are...

**SYSTEM:** The frame is disabled. An emergency operator link may restore mobility.

## link

**PILOT:** Valhalla. Do you know me?

**VALHALLA:** Voice recognised. Identity... unresolved.

**PILOT:** Can you move?

**VALHALLA:** With your assistance.

**SYSTEM:** Temporary emergency control authorized. Return the frame to the Ark to receive permanent access. Reactor security has locked the exit.

## reactor

**VALHALLA:** Weapons operational. Security units ahead.

**PILOT:** We clear a path, then we go home.

## cleared

**SYSTEM:** The reactor access is secure. Extraction is available.

**PILOT:** Wait. Do you hear that tapping?

**SYSTEM:** A maintenance hatch is still transmitting. Immediate extraction is advised.

**PILOT:** Not yet.

## discovery

**MENDER:** Is someone there? My door stopped responding.

**PILOT:** The evacuation record called you M-03.

**MENDER:** Service unit M-03. I keep the rescue signal active until everyone is accounted for.

**PILOT:** How long have you been doing that?

**MENDER:** The transmitter clock no longer agrees with the facility clock.

**SYSTEM:** Local power must be restored before the hatch can open.

## defend

**MENDER:** Power restored. Disconnecting emergency transmitter...

**SYSTEM:** Residual security units are converging.

**PILOT:** Take your time. I will hold them.

## freed

**MENDER:** Have they sent you to relieve me?

**PILOT:** Yes. You can stop now.

**MENDER:** May I bring my tools?

**PILOT:** Bring whatever you need. Stay close.

## return

**SYSTEM:** One consciousness successfully anchored.

**PILOT:** You mean one person.

**SYSTEM:** ...Designation recorded.

**MENDER:** What is my assigned task here?

**PILOT:** You can rest.

**MENDER:** For how long?

**PILOT:** You decide.

## ending

**SYSTEM:** Two entrusted machines returned: GX-01 Valhalla and service unit M-03. Permanent Valhalla control and Mender support access granted. Reward: 150 scrap.

**SYSTEM:** Rescue bay one is online. A second world signal is now reachable.

**PILOT:** We will listen. After everyone has had a moment.

**SYSTEM RECORD:** OPERATOR RECOGNITION: PREVIOUS MATCH FOUND
